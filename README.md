ARSIP PASANG: The Last Dry Record — a top-down 2D stealth game in vanilla JavaScript. Slip through a half-flooded city archive past four patrolling guards, read their vision cones, hide behind tall shelves and low crates, throw pebbles to pull guards away, and listen: footsteps carry twice as far across the puddles. Grab the BUKTI document in the east archive and reach the EXIT unseen.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts --port 8080
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD or arrows to move, Shift to crouch, mouse aim + click to throw a pebble, E to grab the evidence and use the exit, R to restart, Esc to pause, M to mute.
